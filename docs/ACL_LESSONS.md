## Scope and sources

Only the listed finite packets are proved: no arbitrary-host/port policy theorem or automatic TCP session/reply is inferred. This unit teaches host/any selection, ordering, service fields, direction and preservation; arbitrary/noncontiguous wildcard practice, IPv6 ACLs, fragments, NAT, TCP flags/established, counters/logging, real hardware and full IOS behavior remain absent. Empty ACL sections are rejected rather than guessing platform-specific empty-list behavior; a blank whole document removes that router's lists/bindings. Whole-document replacement and virtual interface labels are explicitly native-simulator rules. See [ACL_WORKSPACE.md](ACL_WORKSPACE.md), [ACL_SUBSET.md](ACL_SUBSET.md), [ACL_ROUTING_SUBSET.md](ACL_ROUTING_SUBSET.md) and [ACL_EDITOR_SUBSET.md](ACL_EDITOR_SUBSET.md).
