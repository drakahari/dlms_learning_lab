`lvm-linear-provisioning` follows `lvm-volume-growth`; each new lesson requires
the preceding practical task. The unit teaches PV/VG/LV allocation, whole extents,
raw storage versus filesystems, explicit/implicit PV initialization and cautious
removal/recreation of NEW raw volumes. It does not duplicate existing growth or
backup snapshots. Snapshots, activation, rename/general removal and full204.3
remain open; see [precise supported subset](LVM_PROVISIONING_SUBSET.md).

Introduction supplies a different worked storage plan with named commands/reasons.
Try example uses its own real virtual state, selected command insertion and
explicit Run. Removing its new raw LV invalidates example success; recreate it
and inspect why the check recovers. Insertion never runs a command or awards
credit. Assignment shows requirements, supplied device facts and the ordinary
terminal, current allocation/free space and focused feedback. Worked commands
are not exposed there by default; Challenge keeps the independent full task.

Grading compares actual new capacities, group membership, extent size, reserve,
protected data and absence of unintended allocations. Different supported units,
name/size flag order, explicit/implicit PV preparation and creation order can pass.
Reported output, a completed walkthrough position and saved checked flags cannot
substitute for the resulting state. Current success clears after required storage
is removed, even before another Check. Drafts and examples resume independently.
